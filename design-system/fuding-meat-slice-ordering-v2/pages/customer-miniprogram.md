# Customer Mini Program Override

- Canvas: warm broth cream `#F7F3EA`; product surfaces: `#FFFCF7`; primary: chilli red `#A9402B`.
- Use food photography as the main visual content. Decorative graphics stay subtle and do not compete with dishes.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. Keep checkout/cart action above the safe-area inset.
- Product name, base price and availability appear before marketing description.
- Spec selection uses clear chips with selected checkmarks; do not rely on color alone.
- Pickup number is the largest element on successful order and active-order cards.
- Empty and error states use one short sentence and one useful action. No mascots, robots or conversational assistant copy.
- Avoid nested cards and repeated badges. Use whitespace, dividers and typography before adding containers.
